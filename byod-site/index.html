Bring Your OWN Domain!

Quick setup guide to map your personal custom domain to the server.

🚀 Setup Instructions

  1. Log in to your domain registrar (Cloudflare, Namecheap, GoDaddy, Google/Squarespace, etc.) and open your DNS Management page.
  2. Create a new A record:
    Type: A
    Host / Name: @ (or your desired subdomain like proxy)
    Points to (Value): 159.195.17.92
  3. Save the record and wait approximately 10 to 15 minutes for DNS propagation across global resolvers.
  4. Visit your domain in any browser over http:// or https://!
